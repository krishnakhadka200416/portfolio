## About Me

Hello! I am a PhD candidate at The University of Texas at Arlington researching trustworthy AI. I adapt software testing, verification, and debugging principles to explain, evaluate, and repair machine learning systems. My research spans model interpretability, post-hoc repair, adversarial robustness, and data-centric AI, with oral papers at **NeurIPS 2026** and **KDD 2026** and collaboration with **NIST**. My work includes **8 peer-reviewed papers, 1 manuscript under review, and 70+ citations**.

I am also the co-founder and CTO of **Bhasha Tech**, a language-learning platform serving **70,000+ users across 130+ countries**, where I build personalized learning and conversational tutoring systems.

## Research Interests

My research focuses on **Trustworthy AI Systems, AI Testing and Debugging, Explainable AI, Model Repair, and Computer Vision**.

- **Explainable AI & Computer Vision** -- Minimal sufficient explanations for CNNs and Vision Transformers through delta debugging (DD-CAM, NeurIPS 2026, Oral), and faithful local explanations using adversarial pairs (ABLE, KDD 2026, Oral)
- **Model Debugging & Repair** -- Post-hoc repair of repeated classification failures with decision signatures (DSR, under review at AAAI 2027)
- **AI Testing & Trustworthy Systems** -- Adapting software testing, verification, and debugging principles to evaluate ML systems and study adversarial robustness
- **Combinatorial & Data-Centric AI** -- Synthetic data generation, hyperparameter optimization, surrogate modeling, and tabular knowledge distillation through interaction diversity
